Product

Your depot, on autopilot.

Six things Gridhaul does every night while your team is asleep.

  • Smart scheduling

    Each van gets a charge plan built from tomorrow’s routes. Long routes charge first, short hops last.

  • Load balancing

    Stays under your site’s grid connection automatically. No more tripped breakers at six in the morning.

  • Tariff optimisation

    Charging moves into the cheapest half-hours on your tariff, so you pay night rates for night work.

  • Works with your chargers

    Speaks OCPP 1.6J and 2.0.1, so 40+ charger brands connect in an afternoon. No rip-and-replace.

  • Telematics sync

    Reads state of charge and mileage from your telematics, so plans update the moment a route changes.

  • Cost & CO₂ reports

    Monthly cost, kWh and carbon reports that your finance and sustainability teams will actually read.
